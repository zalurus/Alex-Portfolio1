Design Work

My design work looks dystopian and has a red and black colour scheme. The first line on the design page is typography, and the next line is pattern, and the last line is digital collages. My work features lyrics from songs by artists such as The Verve, TOOL, Radiohead, and Talking Heads, as well as a quote from 1984.

Photography Work

Most of my photos for my year I took of streetlamps and roads. I tried to make my photography feel sort of liminal and empty, and used a lot of grey colours contrasted with bright colours of traffic lights, or red or purple sunsets. The first row of my board, and the first row of the photography page on this website, consists of mirrored photos and tripdics. The second row is collage and double exposure, and the third row is finals, with collages.
